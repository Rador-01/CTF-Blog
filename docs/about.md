## What This Blog Is

This blog is my personal archive. It's a place where I document what I learn, track what I still need to learn, and make sure I actually understand things by explaining them.

But honestly, it's become more than just my personal cheat sheet. I've lost count of how many times a stranger's writeup saved me at 2 AM during a competition. That one paragraph where someone explains *why* a technique works, not just *that* it works, those are the moments that stick with you. If I can pay that forward even once, the time spent writing is worth it.
